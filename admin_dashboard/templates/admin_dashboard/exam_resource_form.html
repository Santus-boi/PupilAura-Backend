Past Questions Entry

This resource represents a collection of examination questions. Create the resource first, then add the questions individually or use the bulk question-entry page.

  • Add the examination year and subject correctly.
  • Enter each question and its answer choices separately.
  • Upload the original examination paper where available.
  • Do not label generated questions as historical examination questions without verification.
Solutions and Answers Entry

This is a separate resource for answers and explanations. After creating it, add questions through the question management page.

  • Enter the question text.
  • Enter the correct answer in the Correct Answer field.
  • Enter the detailed explanation in the Solution field.
  • Optionally link the solution to its corresponding Past Questions question.
