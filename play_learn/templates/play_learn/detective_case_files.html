CONFIDENTIAL CASE 001

The Missing Diamond 💎

At 8:30 PM, the owner of Blackwood Manor discovered that a rare diamond had disappeared from a locked display case. Three people were inside the mansion. The security system recorded a brief power interruption. Your job is to determine who took the diamond, how they did it, and which evidence proves your conclusion.

00:00Investigation time
0/6Evidence inspected
0Investigation points

🔍 Evidence Room

Inspect all six pieces of evidence. Details matter.

Evidence 01 — The Display Case

The diamond was missing at 8:30 PM. The display case showed no signs of forced entry. Its lock was still intact.

Evidence 02 — Security Log

The mansion's camera system went offline at 8:14 PM and resumed recording at 8:19 PM.

Evidence 03 — Muddy Footprints

Several muddy shoeprints were found between the display room and the conservatory. The prints measured approximately size 10.

Evidence 04 — The Conservatory Soil

The conservatory contains a rare reddish clay soil. Fresh traces of the same soil were found on the footprints.

Evidence 05 — The Kitchen Record

The kitchen assistant recorded who entered and left the kitchen during the evening.

Evidence 06 — The Security Key

The security room was accessed using a physical key shortly before the camera interruption.

👤 Suspect Profiles

Question each suspect and compare their statements.

🧑‍💼

Victor Hale — The Butler

Has worked at Blackwood Manor for eight years. Knows the house and its security arrangements.

"I was in the kitchen from 8:00 until 8:30. I never went near the display room. I wear size 10 shoes."
👩

Clara Blackwood — The Niece

Recently argued with the owner about the family inheritance. Had access to the display room.

"I was in the lounge making a phone call from 8:05 until 8:22. You can check my call record."
🧔

Marcus Reed — The Security Guard

Responsible for monitoring the cameras and securing the property.

"I was at the front gate checking a delivery. I did not enter the security room or touch the camera system."

🕒 Investigation Timeline

Reconstruct the evening using verified records.

8:05 PM Clara's phone call begins, according to the phone record.
8:12 PM Victor leaves the kitchen, according to the kitchen assistant.
8:14 PM Security cameras go offline.
8:16 PM Security room key is recorded as being used.
8:19 PM Security cameras resume recording.
8:25 PM Victor returns to the kitchen.
8:30 PM The missing diamond is discovered.
